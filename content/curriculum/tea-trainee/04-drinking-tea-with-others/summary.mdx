<PageContent>

You have mapped who can work in a TEA case and what a public reader sees. A team role manages the team. A case grant determines access to the working argument. Comments let a reviewer question an element. Live updates help open views notice changes. Import and backup move copies of case data; integrations let an authorised service make selected machine calls. Publishing makes a public snapshot on Discover.

Those controls support a process that begins before a case is complete. During deliberation, invite perspectives that can change the goal. During design, turn concerns into strategies and claims. During testing and evidence generation, bring in people who can assess methods and results. Share the working case with the people doing that work, and choose public content only after its limits can be explained.
