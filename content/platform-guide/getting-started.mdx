Before you can build a case, you need an account and a way in.
This page covers the account and sign-in screens, and orients you on the dashboard once you arrive.

## Create an account

Go to `/register` and fill in **Username**, **Email Address**, **Password** and **Confirm Password**, then choose **Submit**.
A successful registration takes you to the sign-in page with a confirmation message; sign in with the credentials you just created.

## Sign in

Go to `/login`.
Enter your **Email or Username** and **Password**, then choose **Login**.

<Callout type="info">
If your deployment has GitHub or Google sign-in configured, the login page also offers **GitHub** and **Google** buttons under "Or continue with". Connecting a Google account can also enable Google Drive features later, such as backing up a case.
</Callout>

Forgotten your password? Choose **Forgot password?** on the login page to request a reset link.

## The dashboard

Signing in takes you to `/dashboard`, labelled **My Assurance Cases** in the sidebar.
This is where your working cases live.

If you have no cases yet, the dashboard offers two ways to start: **New Case** to create one from scratch, or **Import File** to create one from a JSON export, a GitHub JSON file, or a Google Drive backup.
Once you have at least one case, the same choices appear as a **Create new case** card alongside your existing cases, plus an **Import File** button.

The dashboard's sidebar also links to:

- **Shared With Me** (`/dashboard/shared`) — cases other people or teams have shared with you. It shows the same case list as your own dashboard, but only cases where someone else granted you access.
- **Teams** (`/dashboard/teams`) — create and manage the teams you belong to.
- **Trash** (`/dashboard/trash`) — cases you have deleted, awaiting permanent removal.
- **Discover Public Projects** (`/discover`) — assurance cases the community has published.

<Callout type="info">
The first time you sign in, the platform creates a small demo case for you automatically, so the dashboard is never completely empty. It behaves like any other case you own — edit it, delete it, or leave it as a reference while you build your own.
</Callout>
