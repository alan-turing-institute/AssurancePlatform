Welcome to the Trustworthy and Ethical Assurance (TEA) documentation. These pages contain all you need to know about our collaborative platform for building and managing assurance cases.

## Documentation Structure

This documentation is organised into five sections:

- **[Welcome](/docs)** — this page, an orientation for anyone arriving at the documentation.
- **[Platform Guide](/docs/platform-guide)** — for people using the platform: reference material and, in time, feature walkthroughs.
- **[TEA Curriculum](/docs/curriculum)** — for learners: step-by-step learning paths for understanding and applying trustworthy and ethical assurance.
- **[Technical Documentation](/docs/technical-guide)** — for deployers and contributors: setup, architecture, and the CI/CD pipeline.
- **[Community Resources](/docs/community)** — for the community: how to contribute, report issues, and get involved.
